B/01 · Helm chart · Kubernetes · PostgreSQL

pgkube-guard

Most teams back up their databases. Few ever test the restore.

Does
Dumps PostgreSQL to S3 every night, restores each dump into a throwaway database, alerts the moment a backup or a restore fails.
Trade-off
Logical dumps are simple and portable, but you can lose up to a day of data. WAL archiving (what I run at work with wal-g) is the next step for tighter recovery.

B/02 · Self-hosted Kubernetes · Hetzner

keda-observation

Scale consumers on queue lag, not on CPU.

Setup
Go consumers on my own cluster (3 VMs, one control plane), deployed with ArgoCD, watched with Prometheus and Grafana.
Why lag
CPU says a pod is busy; lag says work is waiting. Consumers are added when messages pile up and drop to zero when the topic is quiet.
